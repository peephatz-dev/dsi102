# DSI 102

## Platform
web

## Users and purpose
Thai-speaking DSI 102 students, including juniors, learning digital technology from the eight supplied week folders. They need clear summaries and detailed explanations for every slide topic, source verification, and long uninterrupted reading.

## Capabilities and constraints
React/TypeScript/Vite; Thai and English; original PDFs, interactive educational simulations, review exercises, local learning progress. Preserve the Modhiw Instagram entry flow and existing learning features. Week 7/8 folders contain slides labeled Week 8/9; disclose the distinction. Original slide headings and physical PDF page order are authoritative.
